Projects

Experimental Design of Non-linear Pendulum System Indian Institute of Science, Bengaluru

Developed Python-based motor control using PID and designed 3D-printed experimental components for hardware integration. Built a 1-DOF pendulum rig aimed at the study of non-linear dynamical systems. Additionally, created and optimized a Digital Image Correlation (DIC) framework using OpenCV and computer vision tools for high-precision system state tracking.

Static & Dynamic Analysis of Laminated Composite Plates under Thermal Effects using FEA National Institute of Technology, Silchar, India

Simulated the thermal and dynamic response of laminated composite plates in ABAQUS under various boundary and thermal conditions, gaining foundational expertise in FEM analysis, meshing strategies, and interpreting complex post-processing data from multilayered structures.

Stress Analysis using FEM in a Truss National Institute of Technology, Silchar, India

Developed MATLAB code to analyze truss elements subjected to point and uniformly distributed loads, enabling estimation of element forces, stresses, and strains for structural assessment.

Design & Analysis of a G+4 Residential Building National Institute of Technology, Silchar, India

Developed and analyzed a G+4 residential building model in ETABS, designing beams, columns, and reinforcement in accordance with IS Code standards, including seismic confinement detailing. Conducted wind and earthquake load analyses following IS Code provisions to ensure structural safety and compliance.
