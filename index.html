01About

I am a PhD candidate at Institut Polytechnique de Paris (IP Paris) – École des Ponts ParisTech (ENPC), part of the ERC AUTOMATIX project and advised by Prof. Jeremy Bleyer. My research focuses on scientific machine learning for complex materials — inverse design and constitutive model discovery.

Before joining IP Paris, I worked across academia and industry. I was a Research Associate at the Indian Institute of Science (IISc), exploring AI-driven frameworks and non-reciprocal metamaterials, and collaborated with EPFL’s Data-Driven Mechanics Laboratory on the inverse design of granular architected materials. In industry, I was a mentor at Vizuara AI Labs, where I worked on generative AI models and taught a public course on AI-driven materials.
